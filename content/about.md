Web Writers Collective (WWC) is a space for people who write on the web to discuss the culture of writing online as well as connect and collaborate on projects with like-minded writers.

# Who we are

At WWC we discuss a number of topics centred around the craft of writing, especially online. For example—

* Your writing projects on which you would like some feedback
* The culture of writing on the web: what to share, how to share, whom to share with
* Working through specific challenges, e.g., how to articulate an idea, finding inspiration, working through writer’s block
* Community events you’re participating in, e.g., the IndieWeb Carnival, Blaugust or even Bear Blog Carnival

The list is endless. We welcome people who write across genres including fiction, non-fiction, stories, creative non-fiction, poetry, life writing, personal blog posts and everything in-between.

# Who we are not

WWC is defined as much by what we encourage as by what we do not encourage:

* Sharing your blog set-up or features on your blog
* Announcing or self-promoting your blog posts
* Technical writing or documenting code

While some of these may be allowed in context, we note that plenty of spaces already exist elsewhere on the web for just these activities. At WWC we like to keep our focus on our writing.

# What’s going on at WWC?

## Anthologies

*Anthologies* is out community zine. We are just getting started with it and hope to publish it as issues, possibly quarterly and possibly themed. It will carry contributions from WWC members and, for the time being at least, will be online-only. Like everything else in WWC, the future of *Anthologies* will be shaped by existing members of the collective.

Credit for the name of our zine is due to [Juhis](https://hamatti.org/) who originally came up with ‘Web Writers’ Collective Anthology’. We all really liked it and it stuck.

## Forum

This is our most active space and where our discussions come together. While some parts of our forum are visible publicly, lots more becomes accessible when you become a member of WWC. We like to protect our members’ privacy and confidentiality within the context of WWC and its membership and we therefore restrict access to our *Members-Only* spaces.

Our forum is a fun, engaging and respectful environment in which people can feel confident of being who they are, proud of sharing the work they’ve been doing and excited about potentially collaborating with others. If you are accepted as a member, you’ll find something new here everyday.

# How we function

WWC is community-run, led by our organisers. [James](/authors/james-g/) first brought our community together and [V.H. Belvadi](/authors/vh-belvadi) helped get our website running. Juhis initiated the idea for our forum, following which we set up our [core group of organisers](/organisers), all of who helped greatly in building the foundations of WWC.

All members are free to propose projects, share ideas, and help guide the group while keeping it a fun, safe, thoughtful and interesting place to spend time on the web. Major decisions, including accepting new members, approving policy changes, approving feature additions, running projects, major moderating decisions etc. are overseen by our organisers using a system of majority voting.

# How you can join us

If you are interested in writing, or are someone who writes on the web quite actively, you may like WWC. We believe in the importance of having member-only spaces where people can more freely discuss their writing. This also means our existing members trust our judgement in accepting new applications.

Membership of WWC is therefore by application only. Please fill our short application form and we will get back to you with our decision. If you know an existing member at WWC, they can also propose your membership, but you will still need to submit a filled application form.
